\section{Definitions}

Let $P=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$ and let $f$ bound Byzantine processes. A communication round produces a finite message history $M$ for each receiver.

The abstraction functions are:

\[
A_0(M)=\operatorname{multiset}\{\operatorname{label}(m):m\in M\},
\]

\[
A_1(M)=(A_0(M),C(M)),
\]

where $C(M)$ is the structured authenticated sender/value certificate, and

\[
A_2(M)=M.
\]

Define

\[
M_1\sim_i M_2 \iff A_i(M_1)=A_i(M_2).
\]

An L0 transition factors through $A_0$ when

\[
\exists \widehat T\;\forall s,M:
T(s,M)=\widehat T(s,A_0(M)).
\]

The implementation also models explicit Byzantine sets, receiver-specific equivocation, bounded Heard-Of delivery choices, and problem-specific correctness predicates.
