\section{Theorem Targets and Proven Core}
\begin{theorem}[L0 factorization consequence]
If $T$ factors through $A_0$ and $A_0(h_1)=A_0(h_2)$, then $T(s,h_1)=T(s,h_2)$.
\end{theorem}

The repository machine-checks this theorem and the equivalence laws of $\sim_0$ in Lean.

The stronger research targets $L_0 < L_1$ and $L_1 < L_2$ for selected Byzantine problems are not asserted by the software without matched problem, scheduler, authentication, and adversary definitions.
