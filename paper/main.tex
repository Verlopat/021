\begin{abstract}
We classify synchronous Byzantine algorithms by the information their round transitions may use: message counts only ($L_0^{\mathrm{blind}}$), anonymous value counts ($L_0$), sender-attributed certificates ($L_1$), or full content ($L_2$). Crusader agreement, graded connected consensus and one-dimensional approximate agreement are solvable at $L_0$ with optimal resilience $n>3f$, while Byzantine agreement is the principal $L_0/L_1$ separation candidate. Core lemmas are machine-checked in Lean 4; resilience boundaries are reproduced by exhaustive or sampled model checking.
\end{abstract}
