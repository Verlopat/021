\begin{abstract}
This reproducible software package formalizes a three-level communication-information hierarchy (L0, L1, L2), supplies finite indistinguishability witnesses, and provides hooks for proving problem-specific Byzantine resilience results. General separation and optimal-resilience claims are intentionally represented as theorem targets until the exact problem semantics and adversary assumptions are fixed.
\end{abstract}
